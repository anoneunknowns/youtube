Thue–Morse Sequence: Watch 0 and 1 Weave a Pattern | Num-Num Numbers

Watch the first 256 terms of the Thue–Morse sequence grow into a two-colour woven pattern with our long-tailed Onagadori guide. The positions run from 0 to 255, while each value is only zero or one. Every result is spoken in order.

The formula works on screen for every new term. Begin with t(0) = 0. For an even position, copy the parent value: t(2n) = t(n). For an odd position, swap the parent value: t(2n + 1) = 1 - t(n). The parent position is n, which is not always the position immediately before the result.

The overhead examples connect the same sequence to 0 -> 01 and 1 -> 10, appending a changed copy in the same order, and the parity of the number of ones in a binary position. These are replays and do not add extra terms to the count. The final 16 by 16 textile is read row by row from left to right and contains 128 zeros and 128 ones.

Gentle watercolor illustrations, natural American English, and clear mathematical notation. No background music or sound effects.
