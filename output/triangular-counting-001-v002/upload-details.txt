Count all 61 triangular numbers from T0=0 through T60=1,830 with our meerkat guide in the Light-Pebble Grotto.

Two matching dot triangles form an n by (n+1) rectangle. Taking one of the two equal copies shows Tn=n(n+1)/2. Each new row also adds n dots. All dots are drawn; for larger triangles, follow the row labels and formula. Every result is spoken once in natural American English.
