Count Catalan Numbers from C0 to C30 | Num-Num Numbers

Watch Catalan numbers grow with Tora at the Wind Ribbon Plaza!

We count all 31 indexed values from C0=1 to C30=3,814,986,502,092,304, keeping both initial ones. Ribbon paths show balanced up/down steps that never fall below the starting line. The first-return decomposition explains how smaller choices combine.

For n>=1, Cn=C(n-1)×(4n−2)÷(n+1). Every calculation multiplies first, displays the exact integer product, and divides exactly. The numerical windows show values; symbolic ribbons do not stand for that many physical objects. Natural American English uses the short scale: billion=10^9, trillion=10^12, quadrillion=10^15.
