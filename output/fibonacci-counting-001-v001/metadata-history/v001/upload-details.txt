Watch each new Fibonacci number emerge from the previous two, with our Siamese cat guide above the clouds!

We start at F0 = 0 and F1 = 1, keep both ones, and count all 41 terms through F40 = 102,334,155, the first term above 100 million. Two starting values and 39 additions make 41 indexed terms. For n >= 2, Fn = F(n-2) + F(n-1).

Two number windows enter an addition, a new value appears, and the newest pair moves into the next calculation. Close views show place value and carrying. Natural American English reads each expression prefix and then its result once.

No background music or sound effects. Number windows show numerical values; they do not stand for that many physical objects.
