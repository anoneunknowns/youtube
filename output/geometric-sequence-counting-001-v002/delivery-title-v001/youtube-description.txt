Make all 41 terms of a geometric sequence with our pink rabbit guide at the Glass Gallery of Ratios. Start with 2, and multiply by 3 forty times to reach 24,315,330,918,113,857,602.

Watch the previous value form three equal groups, become the next result, and return as the next input. The first amounts 2, 6 and 18 are shown with exact beads; from 54 onward, labels represent complete group values.

The formula a_n = 2 × 3^(n−1) also works step by step: substitute n=4, find the exponent 3, evaluate 3 cubed to 27, and multiply by 2 to obtain 54. This is a replay of the fourth term. Separate examples compare ratios 2 and 3 and show a shrinking sequence with ratio 1/2. They do not add terms to the main count.

Every main multiplication and full result is spoken in natural American English using the short scale. No background music or sound effects.
