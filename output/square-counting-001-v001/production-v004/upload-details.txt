Square Numbers: Watch the Formula Work | 0² to 100² | Num-Num Numbers

Watch square numbers take shape with our red panda guide at the frozen lake!

We use every whole number from 0 through 100 and multiply it by itself: n² = n × n. All 101 square numbers are counted in order, from 0 to 10,000. Each expression is read, then its result is spoken once in natural American English.

Small squares show one tile per unit. Larger labelled diagrams show dimensions, not individually counted tiles. The final 100 blocks each stand for 100 unit squares, and the zoom key enlarges one existing block. An L-shaped border and a 30+2 area split show how the same rule builds new values.

No background music or sound effects.
