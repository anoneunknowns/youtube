Follow our hedgehog through Collatz Waterworks and watch two rules generate every next value.

For a positive whole number n: if n is even, use n ÷ 2. If n is odd, use 3n + 1. We count every term, without combining steps. The trip from 7 reaches 1 in 16 steps (17 terms). The trip from 27 reaches 1 in 111 steps (112 terms), peaking at 9,232. Each trip stops at its first 1. Continuing from 1 would repeat 1 → 4 → 2 → 1.

These two finite trips do not prove convergence for every positive integer. The general Collatz conjecture remains unproved. Intermediate values such as 81 in 27 × 3 = 81, then 81 + 1 = 82 belong inside a calculation; they are not extra sequence terms. The mirrors show number values, not physical water volumes.
