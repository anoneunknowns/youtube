We count 168 prime numbers from 2 to 997 in natural American English, with a calm pace and large, clear numbers. Tile exhibits introduce factors, show why 1 and 9 are not prime, and explain why 2 is the only even prime. Near 100, we look at why the next prime after 97 is 101.

A prime is a whole number greater than 1 with exactly two positive factors: 1 and itself. One tile in the finished mosaic stands for one prime we counted.
